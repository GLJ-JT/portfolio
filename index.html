Personal statement

I’m Joe, a product designer in London. I spend a lot of time thinking about the tiny pause before someone clicks, books, buys, joins, or gives up.

That pause is usually where the real design problem lives. I like finding what’s missing there: trust, context, motivation, a clearer path, or just a better way to say the thing.

My work moves between research, interface design, systems, and fast prototypes. Different projects, same obsession: make products feel easier to understand, easier to trust, and easier to return to.
